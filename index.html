About

My current academic interests lie broadly at the intersection of language, cognition, and artificial intelligence.

As I am still in the early stage of my PhD, this website is intended to develop gradually alongside my research. I am especially interested in how computational approaches can help us study language representation and processing across both human and artificial systems.
